Branch `task/T07-source-control`. Port of `upstream/apps/server/src/pullRequest/PullRequestService.test.ts`
into `crates/source-control/src/pull_request/service/tests/part_{a..t}.rs`, declared in `tests.rs`.

## Status

Every upstream `it` block is ported, skipped with a reason, or listed as blocked below.
Gate green: `scripts/check.sh -p turin-source-control`, 789 passing, 1 ignored.

## Not ported

Cut host as the subject (GitLab, Azure DevOps, Bitbucket, Forgejo). Named in the PORTING.md
Deviations row for this file. Upstream lines 7112 and 7613 are Azure-only.

Blocked on Rust gaps, not just tests:
- 5725 update-branch refusal. Upstream viewer permissions carry `updateMethods`; the Rust
  `PullRequestViewerPermissions` has no such field, so the service cannot check it. A real
  contract gap, not a test gap. Port after the field exists.
- 4867, 4926, 5006 routed credentials. Need `verify_credential` in `FakeProvider`
  (returns `VerifiedIdentity` and `PinnedCredential`).
- 5628 stack rebase across hosts. Toggles `stackActions` on the capabilities at runtime; the fake
  fixes capabilities at construction.

Not portable to Rust:
- 6244 expired diff released. Relies on V8 GC through WeakRef.
- 7650 agent-credit table, seven rows. Upstream resolves environment and project settings into one
  value. That resolver belongs to the settings layer. Only the service's pass-through is ported
  (`part_t.rs`, 4 cases).

## Known issue (not caused by this port)

`pull_request::read_cache::tests::invalidates_only_the_changed_scope_across_restarts_and_coalesces_its_next_reads`
(read_cache.rs ~L881) fails intermittently under a loaded machine: 7 of 10 concurrent reads on the
restarted cache return the pre-invalidation value `"1"`. Observed 2 of ~8 full `cargo test` runs
while other builds were running. Suspect `CacheInner::get` / `gets.run` coalescing with
`get_cached`'s revision check. Not investigated further.
