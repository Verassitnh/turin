## Known issue (not caused by this port)

`pull_request::read_cache::tests::invalidates_only_the_changed_scope_across_restarts_and_coalesces_its_next_reads`
(read_cache.rs ~L881) fails intermittently under a loaded machine: 7 of 10 concurrent reads on the
restarted cache return the pre-invalidation value `"1"`. Observed 2 of ~8 full `cargo test` runs
while other builds were running; 0 of 8 runs of the test binary alone, and 0 of 11 runs of the module.
Suspect `CacheInner::get` / `gets.run` coalescing with `get_cached`'s revision check. Not
investigated further.
