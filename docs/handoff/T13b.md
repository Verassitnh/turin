## Design decisions

- Pure settlement logic takes `ProjectionSettlementCandidate` (T13a) and `UtcDateTime`s.
  Clock is injected (`SettlementDeps::now_ms`).
- Services that Effect wired as layers are borrowing structs or constructors with explicit
  ports. Retries are explicit loops. Cancellation is future drop.
- Sweeps run sequentially. Upstream's `forEach` concurrency does not change any decision
  the suite checks (see PORTING).
- `DrainableWorker` comes from `turin-shared`. `background_work_holds_completion` comes from
  `projection::shared::pending_background_work` (T13a), not from `turin-shared`.
- `visible_thread_pull_requests` comes from `turin_shared::thread_pull_requests` (T05b).
