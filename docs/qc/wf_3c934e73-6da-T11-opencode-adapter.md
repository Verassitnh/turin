### [low] Continuation with no matching wake sends an empty prompt to OpenCode
- `crates/provider-opencode/src/adapter/v2.rs:` · dimension: logic-fidelity · upheld 1/2
- Evidence: v2.rs:2305-2317: `continues` requires created_by==Agent AND `wakes[session].message_id == message_id`; otherwise it falls through to `self.submit(&session_id, &input, &text, ...)` at v2.rs:2340. ### [medium] Unmatched wake is never discarded, so later idle executions are parked forever
